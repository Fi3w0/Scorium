## Short version

Scorium is free for personal, educational, hobby, and local noncommercial
use. **Commercial use requires a separate written agreement with Fiw Labs.**

If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, treat it as commercial and
contact Fiw Labs before proceeding.

## What counts as commercial use

Examples of commercial use that require a written agreement:

- Embedding Scorium in a product or service you sell, license, or charge for.
- Using Scorium as part of a paid SaaS, hosted service, or platform.
- Distributing a binary or package built from Scorium to customers or users.
- Using Scorium internally to operate a business's production systems where
  reliability support is expected.
- Offering consulting, integration, or support services built on Scorium.
- Anything primarily directed toward commercial advantage or monetary
  compensation.

## What does not count as commercial use

Examples that do **not** require a written agreement (the LICENSE covers
them):

- Personal learning, study, and experimentation.
- Academic teaching and research.
- Hobby projects and personal configuration files.
- Auditing and reviewing the source code.
- Running local, non-public instances for your own testing.
- Forking on GitHub to prepare a contribution back to Scorium (also see
  [CONTRIBUTION_PERMISSION.md](./CONTRIBUTION_PERMISSION.md)).

## How to obtain a commercial license

To request a commercial license, contact **Fiw Labs** through the project's
primary communication channel (the repository's listed contact or issue
tracker). Be prepared to describe:

- The product or service that will use Scorium.
- How Scorium will be distributed (embedded library, binary, hosted, etc.).
- The scale and expected number of users or deployments.

Fiw Labs may grant, refuse, or attach conditions to a commercial license at
its discretion. A commercial license may include redistribution rights,
patent grants, warranty terms, and support commitments that this license does
not provide.

## No implied license

Nothing in the LICENSE, this document, or any other Scorium file gives you an
implied right to use Scorium commercially. If your use is not clearly covered
by the noncommercial grant in the LICENSE, and you do not have a signed
commercial agreement, you do not have permission.

## Reselling and redistribution

Independent binary distribution, package-registry publication (for example
crates.io), and selling Scorium or a derivative are **not permitted** under
the noncommercial license under any circumstances, and require explicit
written permission even within a commercial agreement.
