This document states the trademark and branding policy for Scorium. It works
alongside the [LICENSE](./LICENSE).

## The Scorium mark

"Scorium", the Scorium logo, the `.scor` file extension, the `scorium`
command-line tool name, the `scorium-*` package names, and any related names,
logos, wordmarks, and visual identity (collectively, the "Scorium Marks") are
controlled by **@fi3w0**.

The Scorium Marks identify the official Scorium project. They are not licensed
for unrestricted use by the PolyForm Strict License or by any noncommercial
permission granted in this repository.

## What you may do

Without written permission, you may:

- Refer to Scorium by name in factual, descriptive, and editorial contexts
  ("this project uses Scorium", "compatible with Scorium configuration
  files").
- Use the `.scor` extension and the `scor` code-fence label in your own
  configuration files and documentation that describe or consume Scorium.

## What you may not do

Without written permission from @fi3w0, you may **not**:

- Use "Scorium" or a confusingly similar name for a project, product,
  package, binary, or service that is independent of the official Scorium
  project.
- Claim or imply that an unofficial fork, derivative, or distribution is
  "official", "endorsed", "approved", or "certified" Scorium.
- Adopt a name, logo, or visual identity for a derivative that is likely to
  be confused with the Scorium Marks.
- Register domains, social-media handles, package names, or trademarks that
  incorporate the Scorium Marks, except for branches whose primary purpose is
  contributing back to the official project.
- Present yourself as the maintainer, author, or sponsor of Scorium.

## Official releases and status

Only @fi3w0, or maintainers I explicitly trust in writing, may publish
official Scorium releases, official binaries, or official package-registry
publications. Anything else is unofficial.

## Branding direction

Scorium uses a clean, scientific/material identity. The name is deliberately
neutral and is not water-, fantasy-, or application-themed. Placeholder
branding direction only is provided in the documentation; do not treat it as
a final logo or visual standard.

## Contact

For permission to use the Scorium Marks beyond the allowances above, contact
me directly at @fi3w0 (on GitHub, https://github.com/Fi3w0).
