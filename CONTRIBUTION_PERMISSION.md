# Contribution Permission

This document grants **additional permissions** on top of the
[LICENSE](./LICENSE) (PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0). It is the reason you
can fork, branch, modify, and prepare contributions to Scorium even though
the base license does not allow general distribution.

## What this permission allows

In addition to the rights in the LICENSE, and **without** needing written
permission, you may:

- **Fork** the Scorium repository on a shared development platform such as
  GitHub, for the purpose of preparing a contribution back to the official
  project.
- **Publish branches** whose primary purpose is contributing upstream, or
  sharing patches with the Scorium maintainers for review.
- **Privately modify** Scorium for learning, study, experimentation,
  education, and local noncommercial testing, including keeping those
  modifications for your own ongoing personal use.
- **Share patches** (diffs, branch references) with the Scorium project so
  they can be evaluated for inclusion.
- **Run and audit** the source locally for any noncommercial purpose,
  including inside a single organisation for internal learning and testing
  that is not part of a commercial offering.

## What this permission does not allow

Without separate written permission from Fiw Labs, this permission does
**not** allow:

- **Independent maintained forks** -- public forks kept alive as separate
  projects, rather than as contribution branches.
- **Renamed public distributions** -- publishing Scorium or a derivative
  under a different name.
- **Package releases** -- publishing Scorium (or a derivative) to any package
  registry, such as crates.io, npm, or PyPI.
- **Binary releases** -- building and distributing standalone binaries or
  packaged artifacts to third parties.
- **Mirrors** -- copying the repository to another host for purposes other
  than contributing back.
- **Commercial use** -- any use covered by [COMMERCIAL.md](./COMMERCIAL.md).
- **Selling** Scorium, or using it as part of a paid product or service.

## Boundary: "primary purpose"

The contribution-focused permissions above turn on the *primary purpose* of
the branch or fork. A branch opened to land a feature, fix a bug, or share a
patch with the maintainers qualifies. A public fork advertised as an
alternative Scorium distribution, a maintained independent project, or a
source of binary builds does not, even if it occasionally sends a pull
request.

If you are unsure where your activity falls, ask the maintainers first.

## Relationship to the contributor terms

This document is about what you are allowed to **do** while preparing a
contribution. [CONTRIBUTOR_TERMS.md](./CONTRIBUTOR_TERMS.md) is about the
**rights you grant** when your contribution is accepted. Both apply to
contributing.
